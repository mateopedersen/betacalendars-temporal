# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-06

### Added

- Immutable month and year grids with configurable week starts, fixed or compact layouts, overflow handling, and ISO week metadata.
- Inclusive date-range helpers and bounded recurrence rules for weekday, weekly, monthly-date, nth-weekday, last-weekday, and annual schedules.
- Caller-configurable business-day helpers and calendar-boundary diagnostics.
- Structured grid validation and deterministic JSON, CSV, and Markdown output.
- Offline `betacal` command-line tools for months, years, ranges, and boundaries.
- Automated tests for Gregorian century rules, calendar invariants, serializers, and CLI behavior.
